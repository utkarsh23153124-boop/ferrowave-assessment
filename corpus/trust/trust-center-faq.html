Trust Center: frequently asked questions

Last reviewed 30 June 2026

Certifications and audits

Is Ferrowave SOC 2 certified?
Ferrowave holds a SOC 2 Type II report covering the Security, Availability, and Confidentiality trust services criteria. The report period ends 31 March each year. The report is available under NDA to Scale and Enterprise customers and to prospects in an active evaluation.
Is Ferrowave ISO 27001 certified?
Not yet. Certification is planned; we do not commit to a date.
Do you perform penetration tests?
An independent firm performs an annual penetration test of the web application and API. An executive summary is available under NDA.
Does Ferrowave sign HIPAA Business Associate Agreements?
No. Ferrowave does not currently sign BAAs, and Pulse should not be used to collect protected health information.

Data residency

Where is my data hosted?
Starter and Growth workspaces are hosted in the United States (us-east). Scale and Enterprise workspaces choose between the European Union (eu-west, Ireland) and the United States when the workspace is created. The region cannot be changed after creation; to move regions, create a new workspace and migrate.
Where are backups stored?
Backups are stored in the same region as the workspace.

Encryption

Is data encrypted?
In transit with TLS 1.2 or higher. At rest with AES-256. Keys are managed by our cloud provider's key management service and rotated annually.

Privacy and GDPR

Do you offer a Data Processing Addendum?
Yes. A standard DPA incorporating the Standard Contractual Clauses is available to all customers on request and is included in Enterprise contracts.
Who are your sub-processors?
The current list is in the Data Processing Addendum, Annex 3. We notify customers 30 days before adding a sub-processor.
How quickly do you notify customers of a breach?
Without undue delay and within 72 hours of becoming aware, as set out in the DPA.

Access

Do Ferrowave staff access customer data?
Only for support with the customer's permission, or where required to operate the service. Access is logged and reviewed.
Do you support SSO?
SAML 2.0 SSO on Scale and Enterprise. SCIM on Enterprise.

Availability

What uptime do you commit to?
Enterprise customers receive a contractual SLA with service credits. See the Service Level Agreement for the committed percentage. All customers can view live and historical availability at status.ferrowave.example.
