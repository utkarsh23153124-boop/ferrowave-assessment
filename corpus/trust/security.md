# Security at Ferrowave

Your customers trust you with their opinions. You trust us to keep them safe.

## Bank-grade security

Ferrowave Pulse is built on modern cloud infrastructure with defence in depth at every
layer. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is controlled by the principle of
least privilege, and our engineers are trained in secure development practices.

## Independently verified

We do not just say we are secure; we prove it. Ferrowave undergoes regular independent
audits and penetration tests.

## Built for privacy

Ferrowave is headquartered in Dublin, Ireland, and privacy is designed into the product.
Choose where your data lives, control who can see it, and delete it when you no longer
need it.

## Always on

Our platform is monitored around the clock and engineered for high availability, so your
surveys keep collecting even when your team is asleep.
