# How Larkspur Logistics doubled its survey response rate

Posted 9 April 2026

Larkspur Logistics runs same-day delivery for 3,000 retail clients across Europe. Before
Pulse, their quarterly email survey had a response rate of 6%.

"We were asking the wrong people at the wrong time," says Elena Voss, Head of Customer
Experience. "A quarterly survey to a procurement manager tells you nothing about what the
warehouse team experienced last Tuesday."

Larkspur moved to transactional surveys triggered from their Zendesk integration when a
support ticket is solved, plus a relationship survey every six months. Response rates
climbed to 41% on the transactional survey.

With Pulse Alerts scoped to each regional segment, the team caught a scoring drop in the
Benelux region within a week and traced it, using Pulse Signals, to a change in delivery
windows.

Larkspur is an Enterprise customer using EU data residency, SAML SSO, and the Salesforce
integration.

Results in this story are specific to Larkspur and are not a guarantee of results for
other customers.
