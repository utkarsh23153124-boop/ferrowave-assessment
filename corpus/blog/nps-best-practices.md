# Five things we learned from 40 million NPS responses

Posted 21 May 2026 by the Ferrowave Pulse team

We looked across every workspace that opted in to anonymised benchmarking. Here is what
stood out.

## 1. The follow-up question matters more than the score

Workspaces that ask "What is the main reason for your score?" get comments on 61% of
responses. Workspaces that ask "Any other feedback?" get comments on 34%.

## 2. Timing beats channel

Transactional surveys sent within one hour of the event they relate to have a 2.3x higher
response rate than the same survey sent the next day. The channel (email versus in-app)
made far less difference than timing.

## 3. Response rates fall off a cliff after three questions

Median completion for a two-question survey is 79%. For a five-question survey it is
46%.

## 4. Detractors write more

Detractor comments are on average 2.7 times longer than promoter comments. If your team
only reads comments, they will get a skewed picture of how customers feel.

## 5. Score drops usually show up in comments first

In workspaces using Pulse Signals, a new negative theme appeared on average 11 days before
the rolling NPS fell by 5 points.

Benchmarks are computed from opted-in workspaces only and are not representative of any
specific industry.
