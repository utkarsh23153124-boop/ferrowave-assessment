# An update to Ferrowave Pulse pricing

Posted 3 February 2026 by Aoife Brennan, Head of Product

Since we launched Pulse in 2022 we have shipped Pulse Alerts, the in-app SDK, webhooks,
EU data residency and, most recently, Pulse Signals. Our prices have not changed in that
time. On 1 March 2026 they will.

## What is changing

New prices for new subscriptions from 1 March 2026:

- Starter moves from $19 to $29 per month
- Growth moves from $79 to $99 per month, and its monthly response allowance increases
  from 3,000 to 5,000
- Scale moves from $249 to $299 per month
- We are introducing EUR pricing for customers billed in the European Union

Annual plans continue to be priced at ten times the monthly price, so annual customers
keep the equivalent of two months free.

## What this means if you are already a customer

If you are on a **monthly** plan, your current price stays in place until your first
renewal on or after 1 April 2026. From that renewal you will be billed at the new price.

If you are on an **annual** plan, nothing changes until your next annual renewal. At that
renewal you move to the new annual price. For example, a Growth customer whose annual plan
renews in January 2027 pays the current $790 until then and $990 from that renewal.

The increased Growth response allowance applies to every Growth workspace from 1 March
2026, whether or not your price has changed yet.

Enterprise contracts are not affected by this announcement. Your pricing is defined in your
contract.

## Why

Hosting, model inference for Pulse Signals, and the cost of running EU infrastructure have
all increased. We would rather raise prices openly than quietly reduce what the product
does.
