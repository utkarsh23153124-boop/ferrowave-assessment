# Frequently asked questions

Last updated 2 November 2023

## Do you offer a free trial?

Yes. Every new workspace gets a 14-day free trial of the Growth plan. No credit card
required.

## What is your refund policy?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all plans, no questions asked. Email
support@ferrowave.example and we will sort it out.

## How much does Pulse cost?

Plans start at $19 per month for Starter. See the pricing page for details.

## Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately. Downgrades apply at your next billing date.

## Do you support NPS, CSAT, and CES?

Yes. Pulse includes templates for all three and you can build custom surveys.

## Can I use my own sending domain for email surveys?

Yes. Verify your domain in Settings, Email, and surveys will be sent from your domain.

## Is Pulse GDPR compliant?

Yes. We are based in Ireland and offer a Data Processing Addendum to customers who need
one.

## Do you have an API?

Yes, on Growth and above.

## How do I delete my account?

Workspace Owners can delete the workspace from Settings, Danger zone. Deletion is
permanent.

## Where can I get help?

Email support@ferrowave.example. We aim to reply within two business days.
