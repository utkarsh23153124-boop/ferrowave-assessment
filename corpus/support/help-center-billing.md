# Billing: how it works

Last updated 5 March 2026

## Billing cycle

Monthly plans are charged on the day of the month you subscribed. Annual plans are charged
once a year, in advance. You can see your next billing date in Settings, Billing.

## Invoices

Every charge produces an invoice, available in Settings, Billing, Invoices. Invoices show
the plan, the billing period, extra seats, overage, VAT or sales tax where applicable, and
the total. Amounts on invoices are shown in your billing currency.

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. Enterprise customers can pay by bank
transfer with net 30 terms. Update your card in Settings, Billing.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails we retry on days 3, 5, and 7. You receive an email each time. After the
third failure the workspace is placed in read-only mode until payment succeeds. Data is not
deleted because of a failed payment.

## Changing plans

**Upgrades** take effect immediately. You are charged a prorated amount for the remainder
of the current billing period, and your billing date does not change.

**Downgrades** take effect at the start of your next billing period. You keep the features
of your current plan until then. If your workspace has more members than the lower plan
allows, you must remove members before the downgrade can be scheduled.

You cannot downgrade to a plan whose response allowance is below your current month's
usage until the next cycle begins.

## Cancelling

Cancel from Settings, Billing, Cancel subscription. Your workspace stays active until the
end of the paid period. After that it becomes read-only for 30 days and is then scheduled
for deletion as described in the Data Processing Addendum.

## Refunds

See the Refund Policy. Requests must come from an Owner or Billing Admin and include the
invoice number.

## Why was I charged more than my plan price?

The usual reasons are extra seats added mid-cycle, response overage on Growth or Scale,
the Pulse Signals add-on, or VAT. Open the invoice to see the line items.
