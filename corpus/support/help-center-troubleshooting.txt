FERROWAVE PULSE TROUBLESHOOTING NOTES
Last updated 18 June 2026

------------------------------------------------------------
ALERT DID NOT FIRE
------------------------------------------------------------
Symptom: NPS clearly dropped but no alert email arrived.
Checks:
 1. Plan. Pulse Alerts are on Growth and above. Starter workspaces do not have alerts.
 2. Sample size. A drop alert compares two 7-day windows. Each window needs at least 30
    responses. Low-volume workspaces frequently miss this.
 3. Magnitude. The drop must be more than 5 points. A drop from 42 to 38 does not fire.
 4. Cooldown. A rule that fired in the last 24 hours will not fire again unless the score
    falls a further 5 points.
 5. Scope. Check the rule is scoped to the survey or segment you are looking at.
 6. Spam folder. Alert emails come from alerts@ferrowave.example.

------------------------------------------------------------
EMAIL SURVEYS LANDING IN SPAM
------------------------------------------------------------
Verify your sending domain (SPF and DKIM) in Settings, Email. Avoid sending more than
5,000 emails in the first hour from a newly verified domain.

------------------------------------------------------------
WEB EMBED NOT SHOWING
------------------------------------------------------------
The snippet must be loaded after the DOM is ready. Content Security Policy must allow
scripts from cdn.ferrowave.example. The survey will not show to a respondent who has
already answered it in the last 90 days (configurable).

------------------------------------------------------------
API RETURNS 403 plan_restriction
------------------------------------------------------------
The workspace is on Starter, or the endpoint requires a higher plan (for example,
webhooks endpoints on Growth). Upgrade the workspace.

------------------------------------------------------------
API RETURNS 429
------------------------------------------------------------
You have exceeded your per-minute or per-day limit. Read the Retry-After header and back
off. Remember that limits are per workspace, so multiple keys share one allowance.

------------------------------------------------------------
WEBHOOK SIGNATURE VERIFICATION FAILS
------------------------------------------------------------
If you compute the signature over a parsed and re-serialised body, it will not match.
Use the raw request body bytes. For version 2, include the timestamp: sign
"<timestamp>.<raw body>". Check your clock; requests older than 5 minutes are rejected
by well-behaved verifiers.

------------------------------------------------------------
EXPORT IS MISSING RECENT RESPONSES
------------------------------------------------------------
Exports use UTC. A response submitted at 23:30 local time may be dated the next day in
UTC.
