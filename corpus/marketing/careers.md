# Careers at Ferrowave

We are a team of 62 people in Dublin, Lisbon, and remote across Europe and India. We build
Ferrowave Pulse, the customer feedback platform used by more than 2,000 companies.

## Open roles

- Senior Backend Engineer (Go), Dublin or remote EU
- Product Designer, Lisbon
- Customer Success Manager, EMEA
- Applied ML Engineer, Pulse Signals, remote

## How we work

Small teams, written proposals (we call them RFCs), and a bias for shipping. Every
engineer is on call for the services they own. We meet in person twice a year.

## Benefits

Competitive salary, equity, private health insurance, 27 days of leave plus public
holidays, a learning budget, and a home office allowance.

Apply at careers@ferrowave.example.
