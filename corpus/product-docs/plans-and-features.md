# Plans and features

Last updated 1 March 2026

This article describes which features are available on each Ferrowave Pulse plan. For
prices see the pricing page.

## Collection

All plans include unlimited surveys and all delivery channels (email, link, web embed,
in-app SDK, Slack). Plans differ in the number of responses you can collect per month.

## Team and access

Every plan includes a number of seats. Starter includes 3 seats and cannot add more.
Growth includes 10 seats and Scale includes 25; both can add seats for a per-seat monthly
fee. Enterprise seat counts are set in the contract.

Single sign-on with SAML 2.0 is not available on Starter or Growth. It is available on
Scale and Enterprise. SCIM user provisioning is available on Enterprise only.

## Alerts and analysis

Pulse Alerts, which notify you when your score drops, are included on Growth, Scale, and
Enterprise. They are not included on Starter.

Pulse Signals, our AI theme detection feature, is a paid add-on on Scale and is included
on Enterprise. It is not available on Starter or Growth.

## Integrations and API

Growth and above include full API access. Webhooks are available on Scale and Enterprise.
See the integrations directory for the minimum plan for each integration.

## Data

EU data residency is available on Scale and Enterprise, selected when the workspace is
created. Starter and Growth workspaces are hosted in the United States.

Scheduled exports are available on Scale and Enterprise. One-off CSV exports are available
on every plan.

## Support

Starter and Growth include email support with a two business day target response time.
Scale includes priority support with a one business day target. Enterprise includes a
dedicated customer success manager and a four business hour target for urgent issues.
