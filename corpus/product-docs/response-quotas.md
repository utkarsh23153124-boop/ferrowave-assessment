# Response quotas and overage

Last updated 1 March 2026

## What counts as a response

A response is counted when a respondent submits a score. Partial responses where the
respondent opened the survey but did not submit a score are not counted. Responses count
toward the workspace's monthly allowance regardless of which survey or channel they came
from.

There is no limit on the number of surveys you can create or send on any plan. The
allowance applies to responses received, not surveys sent.

## Monthly allowances

- Starter: 1,000 responses per month
- Growth: 5,000 responses per month
- Scale: 25,000 responses per month
- Enterprise: unlimited, subject to fair use

The allowance resets on your billing date each month.

## What happens when you reach the allowance

**Starter**: surveys stop collecting new responses. Respondents who open a survey see a
polite "this survey is closed" message. Collection resumes on your next billing date or
immediately if you upgrade. Starter does not have overage billing.

**Growth and Scale**: collection continues, and overage is billed automatically at the end
of the billing month. Growth is billed $10 for each additional block of 1,000 responses
(or part of a block). Scale is billed $8 per additional block of 1,000. You can set a hard
cap in Settings, Billing if you would rather pause than pay overage.

**Enterprise**: contact your customer success manager if you expect a large spike.

## Notifications

Workspace Owners receive an email at 80% and 100% of the allowance.
