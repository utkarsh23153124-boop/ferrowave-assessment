# Pulse Signals

Last updated 20 July 2026

Pulse Signals reads the free-text comments in your responses and groups them into themes,
so you can see what is driving your score without reading every comment.

## Availability and price

Pulse Signals is an add-on for Scale workspaces at $149 per month, billed with your
subscription. It is included at no extra cost on Enterprise. It is not available on
Starter or Growth.

## How it works

Signals runs once per day over the responses received in the trailing 30 days. It
produces a set of themes, each with a label, a count of comments, a sentiment split,
and up to five representative comments. A theme needs at least 200 comments in the
window before Signals will report it, so workspaces with low comment volume may see
fewer themes.

Signals models are applied to your workspace's data only. Your comments are not used to
improve models for other customers.

## Languages

As of release 3.5, Signals supports comments in English, Spanish, German, French,
Portuguese, and Hindi. Comments in other languages are counted but not themed.

## Limitations

Signals is a summarisation tool. It does not replace reading comments, and it can group
comments in ways that a human would not. Themes are recomputed daily and labels can
change between runs as the underlying comments change.

## Enabling Signals

Scale workspace Owners can enable Signals from Settings, Add-ons. Enterprise workspaces
have it enabled by default.
