# Getting started with Ferrowave Pulse

Last updated 4 March 2026

Ferrowave Pulse helps you collect Net Promoter Score (NPS) and other customer feedback,
then turn it into something your team can act on. This guide takes you from a new
workspace to your first responses.

## 1. Create a workspace

A workspace is the container for your surveys, responses, team members, and billing. When
you create a workspace on Scale or Enterprise you choose where its data is hosted (EU or
US). This cannot be changed later.

## 2. Build a survey

Pulse ships with templates for relationship NPS, transactional NPS, CSAT, and CES. Each
survey has a score question and an optional follow-up question. You can add up to ten
questions in total, but response rates fall sharply after three.

## 3. Choose a channel

Pulse can deliver a survey through:

- **Email**: Pulse sends the survey from your verified sending domain.
- **Shareable link**: a hosted page you can put anywhere.
- **Web embed**: a JavaScript snippet that shows the survey inside your web app.
- **In-app SDK**: native iOS and Android SDKs plus a JavaScript SDK for single-page apps.
- **Slack**: send a survey to a Slack channel or DM through the Slack integration.

Each channel is available on every plan.

## 4. Watch responses arrive

Responses appear on the dashboard within seconds. The dashboard shows your NPS, the
distribution of promoters, passives, and detractors, and every free-text comment.

## 5. Invite your team

Go to Settings, Team, Invite. Each plan includes a number of seats; see the Seats and
roles article for how seats work and what extra seats cost.
