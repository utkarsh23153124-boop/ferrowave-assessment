# Seats and roles

Last updated 1 March 2026

## Roles

- **Owner**: full control, including billing and deleting the workspace. Every workspace
  has at least one Owner.
- **Billing Admin**: can view invoices, change the plan, and update payment methods, but
  cannot edit surveys.
- **Editor**: can create and edit surveys, view all responses, and configure alerts.
- **Viewer**: can view dashboards and responses but cannot change anything.

## Seats

Every member of a workspace, regardless of role, uses one seat. Pending invitations do not
use a seat until accepted.

Seats included by plan: Starter 3, Growth 10, Scale 25, Enterprise as agreed in your
contract.

## Adding seats

- **Starter**: seats cannot be added. To add a fourth member, upgrade to Growth.
- **Growth**: $9 per additional seat per month, billed with your subscription. Annual
  workspaces are billed $90 per additional seat per year.
- **Scale**: $12 per additional seat per month, or $120 per seat per year.
- **Enterprise**: additional seats are priced in your contract; contact your customer
  success manager.

Extra seats are prorated when added mid-cycle and removed at the end of the cycle when a
member is deleted. If you add seats by mistake and remove them within 24 hours, contact
billing for a full refund of that period's seat charge (see the Refund Policy).

## Transferring ownership

Owners can transfer ownership from Settings, Team. The new Owner must already be a member.
