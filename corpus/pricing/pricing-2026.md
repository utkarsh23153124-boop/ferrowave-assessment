# Ferrowave Pulse Pricing

Effective 1 March 2026. This page replaces all earlier pricing pages.

Ferrowave Pulse is priced per workspace. Every plan includes unlimited surveys, unlimited
questions per survey, and unlimited respondents. What changes between plans is the number
of responses you can collect per month, the number of team seats, and the features unlocked.

## Plans

| Plan | Monthly (USD) | Annual (USD, billed upfront) | Monthly (EUR) | Seats included | Responses per month |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $29 | $290 | EUR 27 | 3 | 1,000 |
| Growth | $99 | $990 | EUR 92 | 10 | 5,000 |
| Scale | $299 | $2,990 | EUR 279 | 25 | 25,000 |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom, annual contract only | Custom | Custom | Unlimited (fair use) |

Annual pricing is equivalent to two months free. EUR prices exclude VAT; VAT is added at
checkout where applicable based on your billing address. USD prices exclude sales tax.

Enterprise is sold on an annual contract only. There is no monthly Enterprise option and
there is no free period on Enterprise contracts. Contact sales for a quote.

## What is in each plan

**Starter** is for a single team getting started with NPS. It includes email surveys,
shareable links, web embed, the in-app SDK, standard dashboards, and email support.
Starter workspaces cannot add seats beyond the three included; upgrade to Growth to add
more people.

**Growth** adds Pulse Alerts, full API access, Zapier and HubSpot integrations, custom
branding, and the ability to add extra seats. Extra seats on Growth are $9 per seat per
month.

**Scale** adds SAML single sign-on, webhooks, scheduled exports, priority support, EU data
residency, and access to the Pulse Signals add-on. Extra seats on Scale are $12 per seat
per month. Pulse Signals is an optional add-on on Scale at $149 per month.

**Enterprise** includes everything in Scale plus SCIM provisioning, Pulse Signals at no
extra cost, 90-day webhook delivery logs, a dedicated customer success manager, a
contractual uptime SLA with service credits, a signed Data Processing Addendum on request,
and custom seat and response volumes.

## Response overage

If a Growth or Scale workspace collects more responses than its monthly allowance, we bill
the overage automatically at the end of the billing month:

- Growth: $10 per additional 1,000 responses (or part thereof)
- Scale: $8 per additional 1,000 responses (or part thereof)

Starter workspaces do not have overage billing. When a Starter workspace reaches its
monthly allowance, surveys stop collecting new responses until the allowance resets or
the workspace upgrades.

## Billing details

Monthly plans are billed on the same day each month. Annual plans are billed upfront for
twelve months. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect
at the start of the next billing cycle. See the Billing help article for details and the
Refund Policy for refunds.

Prices shown are for new subscriptions started on or after 1 March 2026. Existing
customers should read the March 2026 pricing announcement for how the change applies to
them.
