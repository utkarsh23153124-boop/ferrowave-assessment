# Release 3.2

Released 11 February 2026

## New

- **EUR billing.** Customers with a billing address in the EU can now be billed in euros.
  Available on new subscriptions from 1 March 2026 alongside the pricing update.
- **Hard cap for overage.** Growth and Scale workspaces can set a maximum monthly response
  count to stop collection rather than pay overage.
- **Google Sheets integration** is now generally available on all plans.

## Improved

- Dashboard filters now load twice as fast for workspaces with more than 100,000
  responses.
- Email surveys render correctly in Outlook desktop when the recipient uses dark mode.

## Fixed

- Responses submitted in the last second of a billing month were occasionally counted in
  the following month.
- The Slack integration no longer posts duplicate alerts when a channel is renamed.
