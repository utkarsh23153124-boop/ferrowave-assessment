# Release 3.5

Released 14 July 2026

## New

- **Pulse Signals language support.** Signals now themes comments written in Hindi and
  Portuguese, in addition to English, Spanish, German, and French.
- **Comment redaction.** Turn on Settings, Privacy, Redact comments to replace email
  addresses and phone numbers in comments with placeholders in dashboards and exports.
- **Microsoft Teams integration (beta)** on Growth and above.

## Scheduled removal

- **Webhook signatures version 1 will be removed in release 3.6, scheduled for
  15 September 2026.** After that date the `X-Pulse-Signature` header will no longer be
  sent. If your endpoint still verifies version 1 only, it will start rejecting genuine
  events. Migrate to version 2 now.

## Improved

- Alerts evaluate hourly instead of every four hours.
- The API responses endpoint accepts `until` in addition to `since`.

## Fixed

- Exports occasionally included responses from the day after the selected range because
  of a timezone rounding error.
