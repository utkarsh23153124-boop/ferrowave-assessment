# Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 February 2026

Ferrowave Ltd, 14 Fitzwilliam Lane, Dublin 2, Ireland ("we", "us") provides the Ferrowave
Pulse service. This policy explains how we handle personal data.

## Two roles

When you sign up for Pulse, we are the controller of your account data (name, email,
billing details, usage logs). When you use Pulse to collect survey responses from your own
customers, we are a processor acting on your instructions. Our obligations as a processor
are set out in the Data Processing Addendum.

## What we collect about account holders

Name, work email, company name, billing address, payment method details (held by our
payment processor, we see only the last four digits), IP address and browser information,
and how you use the product.

## Why

To provide the service, bill you, secure the service, improve it, and communicate with
you about your account. We do not sell personal data. We do not use survey response
content to train models that are shared across customers; Pulse Signals models are
applied to your workspace's data only.

## Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies for login and session management and a first-party
analytics cookie. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

## Retention

We keep account data while your account is active and for up to 7 years afterwards where
required for tax and accounting. Survey response data is retained as described in the
Data retention article and deleted after termination as described in the Data Processing
Addendum.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your
data, and to object to or restrict processing. Contact privacy@ferrowave.example. You may
also complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission.

## International transfers

Where personal data leaves the European Economic Area we rely on the European Commission's
Standard Contractual Clauses and supplementary measures.

## Changes

We will notify account holders by email at least 30 days before material changes take
effect.
