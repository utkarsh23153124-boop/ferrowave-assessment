# Ferrowave Pulse Refund Policy

Version 3. Approved 10 February 2026. Applies from 1 March 2026.

This policy describes when Ferrowave Ltd ("Ferrowave") will refund fees paid for
Ferrowave Pulse. It forms part of the Terms of Service. If anything in our help articles,
FAQ pages, marketing pages, or community forum conflicts with this policy or the Terms of
Service, this policy and the Terms of Service prevail.

## 1. Monthly self-serve plans (Starter, Growth, Scale)

1.1 New subscriptions. If you start a new monthly subscription and are not satisfied, you
may request a full refund of the first month's fee within **14 days** of the first charge.

1.2 Renewals. Monthly renewal charges are not refundable. You can cancel at any time and
your subscription stays active until the end of the paid period.

1.3 Overage charges are not refundable.

1.4 Additional seats. If you add seats and remove them within 24 hours, the seat charge
for that billing period is refunded in full on request. Seat charges are otherwise not
refundable.

## 2. Annual self-serve plans (Starter, Growth, Scale)

2.1 New subscriptions and renewals. You may request a refund within **30 days** of an
annual charge. The refund is prorated: we refund the unused portion of the annual fee,
less any discount that was applied on the assumption of a full year.

2.2 After 30 days, annual fees are not refundable, but you can cancel to stop the next
renewal.

## 3. Enterprise

Refunds for Enterprise contracts are governed by the contract and by Section 4.2 of the
Terms of Service. Enterprise fees are not refundable except where the contract says
otherwise.

## 4. How refunds are processed

4.1 Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days of
approval.

4.2 Refund requests must come from a workspace Owner or Billing Admin, from the email
address associated with the account.

4.3 We may decline a refund where we have evidence of abuse, such as repeated subscribe
and refund cycles.

## 5. Goodwill

Nothing in this policy stops Ferrowave from offering a refund or credit outside these
terms at its discretion. A goodwill refund in one instance does not create an obligation
in another.

## 6. Contact

Email billing@ferrowave.example with your workspace name and invoice number.
