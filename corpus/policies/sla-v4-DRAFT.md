Reviewer notes are marked with [TF] and [Ops].

This draft proposes extending the SLA to Scale customers and increasing the uptime
commitment following the platform migration completed in June 2026.

## 1. Uptime commitment (proposed)

Ferrowave commits to a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least **99.99%** for the Pulse web
application, survey delivery endpoints, and public API.

[Ops] We have not yet hit four nines for three consecutive months. Recommend keeping 99.9%
until Q1 2027 data is in.

[TF] Sales wants four nines for the Meridian renewal. Parking this until the ops review.

## 2. Scope (proposed)

Applies to Enterprise customers and to Scale customers on annual billing.

[TF] Finance has not modelled credit exposure for Scale. Open question.

## 3. Service credits (proposed)

| Monthly Uptime Percentage | Credit |
|---|---|
| Below 99.99% but at or above 99.9% | 5% |
| Below 99.9% but at or above 99.0% | 15% |
| Below 99.0% | 30% |
