# Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated 5 January 2026

This policy applies to everyone who uses Ferrowave Pulse. It forms part of the Terms of
Service.

You agree not to use Pulse to:

1. Send surveys to people who have not agreed to receive communications from you, or in
   violation of anti-spam laws that apply to you.
2. Collect data you are not permitted to collect, or collect data from children under 16
   without verifiable parental consent.
3. Impersonate another person or organisation in survey content.
4. Upload malware, or attempt to probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the service
   without written permission.
5. Use automated means to extract data from the service other than through the documented
   API, or exceed the rate limits published for your plan.
6. Resell access to the service or share seats between multiple people.
7. Use the service to harass, threaten, or discriminate against any person.

We may suspend workspaces that violate this policy. We will normally notify you first
unless the violation is severe or urgent. Suspension for breach of this policy does not
count as Downtime under any service level agreement.

Report abuse to abuse@ferrowave.example.
