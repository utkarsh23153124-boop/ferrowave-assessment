# Ferrowave Pulse Service Level Agreement

Version 3. Approved by Legal 18 November 2025. Current.

This Service Level Agreement ("SLA") applies to Enterprise customers with a signed order
form referencing it. It does not apply to Starter, Growth, or Scale plans. Scale customers
receive the same operational target (99.9%) but without contractual credits.

## 1. Uptime commitment

Ferrowave commits to a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least **99.9%** for the Pulse web
application, the survey delivery endpoints, and the public API.

Monthly Uptime Percentage is calculated as: (total minutes in the month minus Downtime
minutes) divided by total minutes in the month, multiplied by 100.

## 2. Service credits

| Monthly Uptime Percentage | Credit (percentage of the monthly fee for the affected month) |
|---|---|
| Below 99.9% but at or above 99.0% | 10% |
| Below 99.0% but at or above 95.0% | 25% |
| Below 95.0% | 50% |

Credits are applied against future invoices. Credits are the sole and exclusive remedy for
failure to meet this SLA.

## 3. Claiming a credit

To receive a credit, the customer must email support@ferrowave.example within 30 days of
the end of the month in which the Downtime occurred, including the dates, times, and
affected services.

## 4. Exclusions

Downtime does not include unavailability caused by: scheduled maintenance announced at
least 48 hours in advance (maximum 4 hours per month, outside 08:00 to 20:00 UTC on
weekdays); factors outside Ferrowave's reasonable control; the customer's own equipment,
software, or network; suspension for breach of the Acceptable Use Policy; or beta features.

## 5. Status page

Current and historical availability is published at status.ferrowave.example.
