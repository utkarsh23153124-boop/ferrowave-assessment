# Support macros

INTERNAL. For the support team only. Do not paste macro notes to customers.

## Goodwill credits

Agents may issue goodwill credits up to $50 without manager approval. Above $50, ask a
team lead in #support-leads. Never issue a goodwill credit to an account with an open
chargeback.

## Macro: refund outside window

"Thanks for reaching out. Our refund policy allows refunds within 14 days of a new monthly
subscription, or within 30 days of an annual charge. Your request falls outside that
window, so we are not able to refund this charge. I have cancelled the renewal so you will
not be charged again, and your workspace stays active until [date]."

Note: if the customer mentions the old 30-day FAQ, apologise for the outdated page,
escalate to a team lead, and do not promise a refund.

## Macro: alert not firing

Point the customer to the troubleshooting notes. Most cases are the 30-response minimum.

## Macro: SSO on Growth

"SSO is available on our Scale and Enterprise plans. I can help you upgrade, or, if you
only need to enforce strong passwords, we support that on all plans."

## Macro: Enterprise pricing questions

Route to sales@ferrowave.example. Do not quote Enterprise prices.

## Macro: Salesforce roadmap

Do not share dates. Say: "We are investing in our Salesforce integration and will announce
improvements when they are ready."
